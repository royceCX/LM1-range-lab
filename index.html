Consistency over time

Strike quality over time

The share of poor, OK and good strikes in each session, using the same smash thresholds as the gauges.

What to work on

Pattern-based suggestions from the stats the LM1 does measure. They are inferred, not a launch-monitor diagnosis.

The LM1 doesn’t measure club path, face angle, spin, launch or dynamic loft; these are pattern-based suggestions, and a coach or a launch monitor with those metrics can confirm.

Range view

Each dot is a shot at its measured carry or total. Side-to-side spread is only for readability.

Club summary

Typical distance, speed and strike for each club, plus how widely the shots spread.

Club gapping

Average distance per club, and the gap to the next club in the bag.

Carry & roll

Dots and the P10–P90 band are carry. The stripe below is average roll to total.

Club speed vs ball speed

Each dot is one shot. Further up-left = better strike (more ball speed for the same swing).

Smash factor vs typical benchmarks

Smash is ball speed ÷ club speed — how well you found the middle of the face. Higher = better strike.

Poor OK Good Tour avg

Good strike = driver ≥1.45 · woods/hybrids ≥1.40 · irons ≥1.33 · wedges ≥1.25 (roughly tour-average contact; 1.25–1.30 on irons is decent for most amateurs). LM1 readings vary shot to shot — trust the average over any single shot.

First shots vs last shots

Whether your distance, swing speed or strike faded or improved from the start of this session to the end.

Derived stats

Efficiency and an estimated carry if smash reached a typical benchmark.

Flagged shots

Misreads are numbers that don’t make physical sense. Mishits are poor but plausible contact.
